# Royal heavy attack and atomic on-hit effects

9 October 2026, following deferred-commit checkpoint `6903cc0`. M4/M5 remain **In progress**; M6 has not started.

The existing native hit resolver accepts a bounded typed combat evaluation: direct damage plus at most eight on-hit effect requests. The original damage-only evaluator API wraps the same path. Effect definitions, power and evaluator bindings are prepared before any damage publishes. Direct damage, surviving-target statuses, captured source credit, interruption/death cleanup, hit ledger, commitment/action/outcome queues and the global periodic reservation publish from one candidate batch. Invalid later requests, overflowing queues or periodic capacity leave the earlier damage and ledger unpublished, permitting an exact retry. Direct lethal hits skip statuses. `DamageResult.applied` records direct damage; its final Health/death view includes any instant on-hit effect execution.

Optional `.dakit` `effects` and paired `sources.effects` describe at most sixteen persistent effect assets. Their attribute schema and actor tag registry must match the ability closure. Effect products participate in frozen dependency hashes, kit invalidation and registry/CAS loading. Failed cooks preserve the old published kit; existing leases remain immutable and mixed-generation registries reject preparation. Existing kits without a catalogue retain their previous behavior.

Ashen Roots owns the formula and Burn evaluator in `games/AshenRoots/royal_combat.cpp`. Engine code owns validation, queries, transactions, status clocks and replication. Royal's light ability keeps its original 10-Stamina/25-damage behavior. The Heavy slot uses the existing frozen attack clip, an eight-tick commit marker, a 30-Stamina claim and 35 direct damage. Hold RMB (or the existing Heavy input binding) through windup and impact. Releasing before commitment releases the claim free; releasing after consumption cancels the remaining action without refund. This is a fixed windup, not a scalable charge/release spell.

Heavy damage type 2 applies public Burn to a surviving target. Burn captures source session/network/activation credit and power 5, lasts 180 native ticks, executes every 60 ticks, and refreshes per source. Source destruction does not remove its independent credit; target death removes it. The game formula checks the prepared invulnerability tag. No client damage or status application authority is introduced.

The Royal coordinator consumes authority commitment/outcome queues and prepares the existing public effect frames into read-only persistent cue state. Its accessors expose the latest native step's commitment/cue updates; they do not promise a durable event history across a multi-step render update. Game metrics show reserved Stamina during windup and the public Burn timer while active. Cue visual/audio adapters and RmlUi bars remain separate unfinished gates.
